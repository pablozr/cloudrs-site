cloudrs is a native, open source SoundCloud client for your desktop. No Electron, no webview: drawn on the GPU with GPUI, decoded in Rust.

Side A · Highlights

Eight tracks.
No filler.

Everything cloudrs is about, pressed onto one side. Hover a row to hear it in your head, open it for the liner notes.

  1. 01 Native, for realRust · GPUI · Zed's UI engine GPU 02:14

    No browser inside. The window is drawn on the GPU with GPUI, the framework behind the Zed editor, and the target is under 100 MB of RAM while a track plays. It opens instantly and answers every click at 120 fps.

  2. 02 The waveform is the seek barhover · preview · jump Seek 03:35

    Hover the waveform and the bars up to your pointer light up with a preview of where you'll land. Click to jump. Timed comments will be pinned along the track, right where they were written. (Try the player at the bottom of this page.)

  3. 03 A real queuedrag · play next · undo shuffle Autoplay 04:06

    Drag to reorder, play next, shuffle that you can take back, and endless autoplay from related tracks when the queue runs dry. The row you drag lifts with an accent outline so you always know where it'll drop.

  4. 04 Jam, listen togetherpeer-to-peer · no server New 05:20

    Start a Jam, share a link, and friends hear the same second you do. Only the state travels, over iroh, directly between you. The audio never leaves SoundCloud, and there is no cloudrs server in the middle. More below ↓

  5. 05 Part of your desktopMPRIS · Now Playing · SMTC Soon 03:47

    Media keys and system controls on every OS: MPRIS on Linux, Now Playing on macOS, SMTC on Windows. Your keyboard's play key just works.

  6. 06 Keyboard firstCtrl K · Space · paste a link Keys 01:31

    CtrlK to search, Space to play, and paste any SoundCloud link anywhere in the window to play it right away.

  7. 07 Your librarylikes · playlists · feed · following Sign in 05:01

    Sign in through SoundCloud's own web sign-in, and your likes, playlists, feed and followings are there. The token lives in your OS keychain, never in plain text.

  8. 08 Remembers where you werequeue · position · volume Restore 04:44

    Close it mid-song. Open it tomorrow. The queue, the position and the volume come back, paused, right where you left them.

New Jam

Same song.
Same second.
No server.

Start a Jam, share a link, and your friends' players follow yours. Peers connect directly over iroh. Only the state travels between you; every player streams its own audio from SoundCloud.

  1. 1
    Host a Jam. Your player becomes the authority: track, position, play and pause.
  2. 2
    Share the link. Friends paste it anywhere in cloudrs to join.
  3. 3
    Stay in sync. Clock offset and drift correction keep everyone on the beat.
invite cloudrs://jam/ example
  • 4–9 ms to connect, direct
  • 0.000 s idle CPU in 30 s
  • ~27 B/s per guest

Architecture

A clean signal chain.

A Rust workspace patched like a modular rack: every crate is one module with one job, cables only run one way, and the tests fail if anyone patches around them.

Get started

Three commands
to your first track.

Stable Rust is all you need. On Linux, install the audio and windowing packages listed in the README first.

~/code

$ git clone https://github.com/pablozr/cloudrs
$ cd cloudrs

# run the desktop app
$ cargo run -p cloudrs
